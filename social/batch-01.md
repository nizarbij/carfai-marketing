## After posting

Log every post in `.agents/skills/impeccable/assets/content-log-template.csv`. Without it, there's nothing to analyze in two weeks and iteration becomes guesswork.

Fill these at **24 h** and again at **7 days**: views, completion rate, avg watch time, profile visits, follows gained.

**What to look for after all five are up:**
- Which **pillar** performed, not which video. One video is noise.
- Which **hook shape** held attention past 3 seconds.
- Whether the **demo** posts (CF-001, CF-003) beat the **talking** posts. If they do — and they usually do for an unknown app — the next batch is mostly demos.

Retry the Instagram link field and the TikTok website field after these are live. Both restrictions usually lift once an account has real content.
