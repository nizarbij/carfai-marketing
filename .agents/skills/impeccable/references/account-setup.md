# CARFAI — account setup, step by step

> Phase 1 reference. Paste-ready copy lives in `assets/profile-copy.md` — keep it open alongside.
> Platform UIs move. Paths were current 2026-08-23; if a label has moved, search the **setting name**, not the path.
>
> **Current scope (2026-08-23): EN only.** Three properties — TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Page — all on the single address `carfai.info@gmail.com`, created on **desktop**, in one sitting.
>
> The unique-email and paced-creation rules live in § *Adding the FR / ES / AR accounts later* at the bottom. They do **not** apply to one account per platform. One email works fine across three different platforms; the constraint is only about multiple accounts on the *same* platform.

## Before you start — 5 minutes

**1. Have the images ready.** Generated by `npm run gen:social` into `carfai-marketing/output/social/`:

```
avatar-tiktok-200.png
avatar-instagram-320.png
avatar-facebook-360.png
cover-facebook-1640x856.png
```

**2. Have `assets/profile-copy.md` open** in a second window for the strings.

**3. Enable 2FA on each account as you create it** — not later. A hijacked brand account with a Play Store link in its bio is a phishing vector aimed at your own users. Authenticator app over SMS wherever offered.

## Step 1 — Facebook Page (~10 min)

Do this first. Instagram's Business conversion offers to link a Page mid-flow, and having it ready avoids a re-do.

1. **facebook.com**, logged in as yourself → sidebar → **Pages** → **Create new Page**
2. **Page name**: `CarFai`
3. **Category**: type `App Page` → select. Add `Product/Service` as secondary.
4. **Bio**: the 239-char short description from `assets/profile-copy.md`
5. **Create Page**
6. **Profile picture**: `avatar-facebook-360.png`
7. **Cover photo**: `cover-facebook-1640x856.png`
8. **Edit Page info**:
   - Username: `carfai.official` → gives `facebook.com/carfai.official`
     > Facebook rejects usernames containing a TLD-like string (`.app`, `.com`), so `carfai.app` is refused there. TikTok and Instagram accept it fine. A mismatch on the Facebook URL costs ~nothing: nobody types a Page URL.
   - Website: `https://carfai.app`
   - Email: `carfai.info@gmail.com`
9. **Settings** → confirm the Page is **published**

**Do not post promotional content into driver Groups from this Page.** Group strategy is Phase 2, and most driver groups ban self-promo outright — read `references/markets.md` § Facebook first.

## Step 2 — Instagram (~10 min)

1. **instagram.com** → **Sign up** → email `carfai.info@gmail.com`
2. **Username**: `carfai.app` (fallbacks in profile copy if taken)
3. **Full name**: `CarFai · Car Cost Tracker`
4. Verify the emailed code. Skip contact-import and follow-suggestion screens.
5. **Edit profile** → profile photo → `avatar-instagram-320.png`
6. **Bio** — type it with real line breaks (press Enter between lines):
   ```
   A second opinion for your car.
   Scan a receipt → AI logs the cost.
   OBD2 codes in plain language.
   Free tier, no card.
   ↓ Get it on Google Play
   ```
   Web occasionally flattens a multi-line paste. If that happens, type the breaks manually or do this one field on the phone app.
7. **Links** → **Add external link** → `https://carfai.app`
8. **Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account**
   - Category: **App Page**
   - Choose **Business**, not Creator — Business gets Meta Business Suite scheduling
   - Link the **CarFai** Page from Step 1 when prompted
   - If web doesn't surface the switch, do this single step in the mobile app
9. **Settings → Accounts Center** → confirm Page + IG are linked
10. **Settings → Password and security → Two-factor authentication** → authenticator app

## Step 3 — TikTok (~15 min)

1. **tiktok.com** → **Sign up** → **Use phone or email** → **Email** → `carfai.info@gmail.com`
2. **Date of birth: your real one.** It sets ad eligibility and is painful to correct.
3. Verify the emailed code.
4. TikTok auto-assigns a random username. Change it: **Profile → Edit profile → Username** → `carfai.app`
   > ⚠️ Username changes are allowed only **once every 30 days**. Get it right now.
5. **Switch to Business — before posting anything**
   Profile picture (top right) → **Settings** → **Account** → **Switch to Business Account** → Category: **Apps**
6. **Edit profile**:
   - Name: `CarFai · Car Cost Tracker`
   - Bio: `A second opinion for your car. Scan receipts · read OBD2 · ask anything.`
   - Website: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.carfai.app`
     — Business accounts get the bio link immediately; that's the entire reason for choosing Business over Creator
   - Photo: `avatar-tiktok-200.png`
7. *(Optional)* **Settings → Content preferences → Video languages** → English
   Buried, renamed often, and missing entirely on some builds — **skip it if you cannot find it in 30 seconds.** It mostly controls which languages appear in *your own* feed; its effect on who sees *your* posts is weak and indirect. What actually drives distribution language is the caption, the on-screen text, and the spoken audio — all English by default here.
8. **Settings → Security → 2-step verification** → on
9. **Settings → Privacy** → account **public**, **"Suggest your account to others"** on
10. **Post one video.** An empty profile reads as abandoned to both the algorithm and to visitors. The scene-1 clip from `docs/TIKTOK_PRODUCTION.md` works, or a 20-second screen recording of a receipt scan.

**Business account music constraint**: you're now limited to the Commercial Music Library — no trending pop sounds. That's the accepted trade for the bio link. CarFai's format (screen recording + VO + on-screen text) doesn't lean on trending audio.

**Desktop tooling**: `tiktok.com/tiktokstudio` handles uploads, scheduling (up to 10 days out), and analytics. Business Suite is under your profile picture → **Business Suite**.

## What not to do

- **Don't buy followers.** It permanently poisons the audience signal and makes your own analytics useless.
- **Don't cross-post an identical caption.** Meta suppresses duplicate cross-posted copy. One asset, three tailored captions — see `references/platform-specs.md`.
- **Don't show a clickable App Store badge** until Apple approves. Play only right now.
- **Don't run the accounts through a VPN or proxy tool.** Normal home connection.

## Adding the FR / ES / AR accounts later

Only when the trigger in `references/markets.md` fires — EN posting for 4+ weeks without a gap, one repeatable format, and spare capacity.

**Then these two rules start applying:**

**1. Unique email string per TikTok account.** TikTok rejects a second account on an email it's already seen. Gmail plus-addressing gives you distinct strings on the same inbox — no new mailbox, no new password:

```
carfai.info+tt.fr@gmail.com   → TikTok FR
carfai.info+tt.es@gmail.com   → TikTok ES
carfai.info+tt.ar@gmail.com   → TikTok AR
```

If TikTok rejects `+` addresses, dot-variants also collapse to one Gmail inbox: `car.fai.info@`, `c.arfai.info@`, `ca.rfai.info@`.

Record which address maps to which account as you go.

**2. One account per day, not one sitting.** Creating several accounts from one device back-to-back is the pattern TikTok's abuse detection flags — it fingerprint-links them, and later a strike against one endangers the rest. Post something from each before creating the next.

Per-account differences:

| | FR | ES | AR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Username | `carfai.fr` | `carfai.es` | `carfai.ar` |
| Name | `CarFai · Frais auto` | `CarFai · Gastos del auto` | `CarFai · مصاريف سيارتك` |
| Video language | French only | Spanish only | Arabic only |
| Bio | from `assets/profile-copy.md` | ditto | ditto |
